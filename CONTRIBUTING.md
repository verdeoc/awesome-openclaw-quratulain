# Contributing to Awesome OpenClaw

Thanks for your interest in contributing! This list grows through the community.

## Adding a resource

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Add your link** under the most relevant category in `README.md`.
3. **Format:**
   ```
   - [Name](URL) — One-sentence description ending with a period.
   ```
4. **Open a pull request** with a short description of what you're adding and why it belongs.

## What gets accepted

- ✅ Plugins published on npm or ClawHub
- ✅ Skills, templates, and starter projects with clear setup instructions
- ✅ Tutorials, blog posts, or videos that teach OpenClaw concepts
- ✅ Tools that help OpenClaw developers (debuggers, generators, linters)
- ✅ Active community resources (forums, Discord/Slack channels, conferences)

## What does not get accepted

- ❌ Unmaintained projects (no commits in 12+ months)
- ❌ Self-promotional content without clear OpenClaw value
- ❌ Paid courses or products without a free educational component
- ❌ Duplicates of already-listed resources
- ❌ Broken or dead links

## Quality guidelines

- **One link per PR.** Keeps reviews fast and merges clean.
- **Alphabetical or chronological order** within a section, unless the section explicitly notes another order.
- **No emoji-laden descriptions.** Plain, factual one-sentence descriptions.
- **Test your link.** Make sure it loads and points to the right place.
- **English only** for descriptions. Linked content can be in any language — note the language in the description if not English.

## New categories

If your resource doesn't fit an existing category, propose a new section in your PR description. Maintainers will decide whether to merge it as a new category or place the link under an existing one.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagreements about whether a link belongs are normal — discuss in the PR, not in personal channels.

## Maintenance

Submitted resources may be removed in the future if:

- The project is abandoned or archived.
- The link breaks and is not fixed within 30 days of a removal notice.
- The resource is found to be misleading, malicious, or out of scope.

Removal is not personal — it keeps the list trustworthy.
